# Abandoned But Not Forgotten

An interactive 3D website that tells the stories of NASA spacecraft left on
the Moon and Mars, such as Apollo's Eagle and Intrepid, Surveyor 3, Spirit,
Opportunity, Pathfinder, Viking 1, InSight and Ingenuity.

Spin a 3D Moon or Mars, tap a vehicle on its surface, and read its story,
from landing day to its final signal. The project turns dry technical
mission data into short stories so beginners and young space fans can
enjoy them.

## Features
- 3D Moon and Mars built with Three.js, using real NASA surface imagery
- Small 3D vehicle models you can rotate and inspect
- Story cards for 10 vehicles (4 on the Moon, 6 on Mars)
- Turkish and English versions

## Disclaimer
This is an educational project and is not an official NASA website.
Vehicle models are simplified drawings and vehicle positions are approximate.

## Sources
NASA NSSDCA and NASA Science mission pages.
