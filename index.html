Abandoned But Not Forgotten

The machines left on the Moon and Mars aren’t junk, they’re heroes with stories still to tell. Spin them around in 3D and read their stories.

NASA STORIESMoon · Mars

Drag to spin the planet, then tap a small vehicle on its surface or pick one below.

This is not an official NASA website; it is an educational project based on NASA sources. The Moon’s surface uses real images from NASA’s LRO spacecraft and Mars’s surface uses images from NASA’s Viking orbiters. Vehicle models are simplified drawings, and the vehicles’ positions are approximate. Sources: NASA NSSDCA and NASA Science mission pages; extra details from public mission records.
